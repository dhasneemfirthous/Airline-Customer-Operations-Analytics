# ✈️ Airline Customer & Operations Analytics Dashboard

### Power BI | SQL | Python | Data Analytics

An interactive airline analytics project designed to explore customer behavior, revenue performance, flight operations, and business insights using simulated airline data.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Airline Customer & Operations Analytics Dashboard** is a data analytics portfolio project that transforms simulated airline data into meaningful business insights through interactive Power BI visualizations.

The project focuses on analyzing airline performance across customer preferences, revenue trends, flight operations, booking channels, and route performance. It demonstrates how data analysis and business intelligence tools can help organize complex data, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

This project showcases practical knowledge of **Power BI, SQL, Python, data cleaning, data validation, KPI reporting, and dashboard development**.

> **Data Disclaimer:** This project uses simulated data for educational and portfolio purposes. It is not based on Emirates' or any other airline's internal or confidential data.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze revenue performance and passenger trends.
* Understand customer travel behavior and preferences.
* Evaluate flight operations and delay patterns.
* Explore route performance and travel demand.
* Monitor key performance indicators using interactive dashboards.
* Identify patterns that may support business planning.
* Present analytical findings through clear and interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard Pages

This project contains four dashboard pages, each designed to explore a different aspect of airline performance.

### 1. Executive Overview

![Executive Overview Dashboard](dashboard-screenshots/Executive_Overview.png)

The Executive Overview provides a high-level summary of airline business performance through key metrics and visualizations.

**Focus areas:**

* Overall revenue and passenger performance
* Flight performance indicators
* Revenue and operational trends
* Summary KPIs for business monitoring
* Interactive filters for exploring the data

**Purpose:** Help users understand the overall performance of the simulated airline dataset from a single dashboard.

### 2. Customer Insights

![Customer Insights Dashboard](dashboard-screenshots/Customer_Insights.png)

The Customer Insights page explores passenger characteristics, travel preferences, and customer behavior.

**Focus areas:**

* Passenger demographics and customer segments
* Travel purpose and booking behavior
* Passenger preferences and travel patterns
* Customer-related performance indicators
* Comparisons across available customer categories

**Purpose:** Understand passenger patterns and identify customer segments that may be useful for business analysis.

### 3. Operations Dashboard

![Operations Dashboard](dashboard-screenshots/Operations.png)

The Operations page focuses on flight performance and operational efficiency.

**Focus areas:**

* Flight status and operational performance
* Flight delays and delay patterns
* Average delay analysis
* Origin and route comparisons
* Operational KPIs and performance trends

**Purpose:** Examine operational patterns and highlight areas that may require further investigation.

### 4. Business Insights

![Business Insights Dashboard](dashboard-screenshots/Business_Insights.png)

The Business Insights page brings together analytical findings to support business-oriented decision-making.

**Focus areas:**

* Revenue performance across available categories
* Revenue by cabin class and destination
* Travel purpose and booking channel analysis
* Monthly revenue trends
* Route-level performance comparisons

**Purpose:** Explore business performance patterns and identify opportunities for further analysis using the simulated dataset.
